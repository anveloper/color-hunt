## 공통 네거티브 기준

- no watermark
- no camera icon
- no glossy startup gradient
- no mascot
- no dark background
- no photoreal mockup
- no fake brand text other than specified text

## 1. 로고 안 1

컨셉: 가장 낙서 같고 빠르게 툭 쓴 느낌. 네 자음이 각각 독립적이지만 한 줄 리듬으로 이어져야 한다.

```text

Asset type: app logo exploration
Primary request: create a playful handwritten Korean consonant logo for a mobile web app called Color Hunt, using the shorthand ㅋㄹㅎㅌ as the main brand mark for a social activity where people collect photos dominated by one color and arrange them into a grid collage
Scene/backdrop: plain paper-like off-white background
Subject: the handwritten shorthand logo "ㅋㄹㅎㅌ" written in one energetic rhythm, with each consonant slightly tilted and slightly different in color
Style/medium: colored pencil handwriting, casual doodled branding, clean editorial logo presentation, vector-friendly silhouette
Composition/framing: centered composition, logo only, generous negative space
Lighting/mood: flat clean presentation, warm and lively
Color palette: off-white paper, charcoal ink, muted pencil red, orange, teal, blue
Materials/textures: light paper grain, soft colored pencil texture, subtle hand-drawn wobble
Text (verbatim): "ㅋㄹㅎㅌ"
Constraints: must feel spontaneous but not messy, legible at small size, no extra objects, no watermark
Avoid: photorealism, 3D, UI screenshot, childish cartoon faces, heavy decoration
```

선택 기준:
- 가장 손글씨 같다
- 4글자 리듬이 자연스럽다
- 축소해도 글자 덩어리가 무너지지 않는다
